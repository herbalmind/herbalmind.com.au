What is mugwort?

Mugwort is a common name used for several plants in the Artemisia genus. The exact species, ingredient list and intended use will be specified for each final product.

Where can I find product and safety information?

No. Contact us for the confirmed ingredient list, intended use and safety information for a specific product. Our botanical collection is not presented as a treatment or cure for medical conditions.
